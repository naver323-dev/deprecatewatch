Privacy

This site is static. It has no accounts, no forms, no advertising and no third-party scripts other than the aggregate analytics tag described below.

Analytics

Pageviews are counted with Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-SYFR20K6B2) with IP anonymisation enabled. The tag records the page path, referrer, an approximate location derived from the request and basic device information. It is used only to see which pages are read. No advertising or remarketing data is collected, and the data is not sold or shared for advertising.

Cookies

Google Analytics may set a first-party cookie on this domain to distinguish sessions. No other cookies are set by this site.

Third parties

Fonts, stylesheets and scripts are served from this domain, except the Google Analytics tag from googletagmanager.com. Links to vendor documentation and to endoflife.date leave this site and are governed by those sites' own policies.

Data requests

Questions about analytics data or removal requests can be sent to hello@deprecatewatch.pages.dev.
